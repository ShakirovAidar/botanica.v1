## Developer advantages and trust points

Use these as a separate “Why Botanica” section rather than repeating them in every project card.

- Over 31 successfully developed and sold projects, according to the developer’s website.
- More than a decade of experience creating luxury pool villas in Phuket.
- A portfolio spanning entry luxury, mid-luxury and ultra-luxury villa segments, plus condominium living.
- Distinctive architectural concepts with an emphasis on innovation, quality and functional luxury.
- Modern tropical, Balinese, Zen and loft-inspired design languages adapted to Phuket’s climate and lifestyle.
- Natural-light, indoor-outdoor and landscape-led planning across multiple projects.
- Residence management and after-sales services are offered by the developer.
- Botanica Privilege Club benefits include partner offers across dining, spa, wellness, pet care and car care.
- The developer also offers rental services for short-term luxury stays.
- Promotions may include price reductions, free home maintenance or smart-home installations; availability must be checked per project.

Avoid presenting “award-winning”, “over 31 projects” or any payment/price claim as universal for every project. Attach those claims only where the current official project material confirms them.
